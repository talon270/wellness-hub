# Yellow Dude workout suggestions

Research snapshot: 6 October 2026. This is a shortlist of **workout structures and focused practice ideas** drawn from the channel research, for choosing what might later belong in Wellness. Exercise IDs link to the [218-item selection catalog](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercises.md); movement requirements and setup checks are in the [exercise cards](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md). The [training notes](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-training-advice-notes.md), [foundations notes](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-foundations-advice-notes.md), and [later advice notes](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-later-advice-notes.md) preserve the per-video context.

**Source labels:** C = YouTube auto-captions; A = local speech recognition, with greater uncertainty about exact words and numbers. A named guest's advice is attributed to the guest. These are creator examples, not a personalized program or a claim that an app prescription has been reviewed. The app's current workout rules live in `TRAINING_DATA`; nothing here changes them. The [CDC adult guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html) supports spreading activity through the week and strengthening major muscle groups on at least two days; the [ACSM 2026 summary](https://acsm.org/resistance-training-guidelines-update-2026/) stresses consistency, individual goals, and manageable bodyweight or band work rather than a single ideal template.

## How to choose a suggestion

| If the user wants… | Start with… | Selection and logging detail |
| --- | --- | --- |
| A general strength routine | [Full-body foundation](#full-body-foundation) | Pick one manageable push, pull, squat or lunge, hinge, and core exercise. |
| A repeatable week | [Ways to arrange the week](#ways-to-arrange-the-week) | Choose a frequency that fits recovery and time available. |
| A shorter session | [Compact full-body session](#compact-full-body-session) | Reduce the number of sets before removing the pull or leg pattern. |
| Training without an overhead bar | [No-bar home session](#no-bar-home-session) | Confirm that any row support can bear the intended load. |
| A specific movement goal | [Focused practice](#focused-practice) | Keep skill work optional and separate from whole-body coverage. |
| Extra work for one area | [Optional accessory blocks](#optional-accessory-blocks) | Add one or two suitable choices after the main session. |
| A push-up follow-along | [Push-up video series](#push-up-video-series) | The videos cover pressing, not a whole-body session. |
| Conditioning | [Conditioning choices](#conditioning-choices) | Record time, distance, effort and recovery rather than strength reps. |
| A short trunk or maintenance sequence | [Core and maintenance examples](#core-and-maintenance-examples) | Treat creator challenges as optional examples with their own recovery needs. |
| Easy movement between sessions | [Mobility flow](#mobility-flow) | Use the Mobility routine's existing completion record. |

Before recommending any version, check actual equipment and setup, a comfortable starting movement, recent training and symptoms. A gear toggle cannot establish that a table, door, chair, bar or anchor is secure. Stop a movement that causes pain; persistent symptoms or conditions affecting exercise choice call for individual guidance. Advanced inversions, levers, high-impact jumps and maximal sprints need their own preparation and review.

## Full-body foundation

**Creator/guest example, C.** In [#1, guest Alex from Cali Move](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mn2jfbtDPZk&t=87s), the example session covers push-ups, rows, lunges, hollow-body crunches and arch-ups for three sets each. He suggests two or three full-body days with recovery between, about two minutes' rest, and one or two clean reps left on most sets. The [warm-up](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mn2jfbtDPZk&t=29s) raises body temperature, moves the main joints, then rehearses easier versions of the session's exercises. His rule about moving on after three sets above 15 clean reps is a coaching example, not a universal advancement test. [#20](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=C1DDPRYXN5s&t=158s) also calls for balancing pushing with pulling and squat work with a hip hinge.

**Candidate Wellness assembly, editorial:**

1. Warm up with light movement and easy versions of today's exercises.
2. Choose one push, such as [P01 wall](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#p01), [P02 high incline](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#p02), or [P08 floor push-up](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#p08).
3. Choose one pull, such as [R20 inverted row](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#r20) or [R08 assisted pull-up](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#r08), only with suitable gear.
4. Choose one leg exercise, such as [L01 squat](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#l01) or [L05 split squat](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#l05), and a manageable hinge such as [L25 glute bridge](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#l25).
5. Choose one core exercise, such as [C01 plank](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c01) or [C05 hollow hold](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c05).

This assembly adds an explicit hinge to the guest's five-exercise example so the app's existing movement coverage is represented. Sets, ranges and rest must come from reviewed app rules for the *chosen* variations; do not copy the guest's three sets onto every beginner or skill card. The channel's other beginner outline [#101, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=euMOF0Vwliw&t=232s) gives two or three sets of roughly 8–12 for push-ups, assisted/full pull-ups, dips and squats, but its dips need a separate readiness and apparatus check and its proposed three or four weekly days differ from #1.

## Ways to arrange the week

These are examples from different uploads, with different goals. A missed day does not need to be made up by doubling the next session. The app should let the user pick a feasible schedule and adapt it to actual recovery.

| Source example | Arrangement described | Limit for a suggested plan |
| --- | --- | --- |
| [#1, guest Alex, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=Mn2jfbtDPZk&t=29s) | Two or three full-body sessions weekly with recovery between. | The exercise variation and workload still depend on current ability. |
| [#53, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0uiGPnSNXgQ&t=330s) | Three 20–30-minute sessions distributed through the week. | A time target is a convenience, not a required minimum. |
| [#61, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PwcgXY2AZk&t=300s) | Hypothetical Monday/Wednesday/Friday strength, Tuesday/Saturday short rope intervals, Thursday/Sunday walking or mobility. | It is a fat-loss-themed example; neither intervals nor that exact seven-day pattern guarantees fat loss. |
| [#91, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=36bkuPKwvbU&t=270s) | Three to five training days depending on goal and recovery, with easier days or rest between hard work for the same muscles. | Avoid a fixed 48-hour gate or interpreting every walk as a hard session. |

For a new general-strength suggestion, the two-day starting option is compatible with the [CDC's adult muscle-strengthening guidance](https://www.cdc.gov/physical-activity-basics/guidelines/adults.html). More days can be offered as a user choice after workload and recovery are known. The [#34 muscle-building template](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JR59JZxxt5U&t=30s) is another creator-specific example: two or three days, four to six compound movements and longer rest between hard sets. Its stated weekly hard-set target is a goal-specific proposal, not a default for a beginner.

## Compact full-body session

**Creator examples, C.** [#11](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwYnurXeuqM&t=102s) offers three seven-minute blocks across the day, and a short home circuit with a push-up, squat, plank, dip, and bridge or lunge variation ([circuit](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=UwYnurXeuqM&t=451s)). It mentions about 10–15 reps or a 30-second plank, but does not establish a common set count for every variation. [#55](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=glAa9ntJHvE&t=210s) sketches a 30-minute session: three minutes' dynamic warm-up, 15–18 minutes of compound push/pull/leg work, six to eight minutes of accessory/core work, and two or three minutes to cool down. [#17](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=J2KfUlg1SJ0&t=314s) suggests brief focused rounds three times weekly, shortening rounds or resting longer when clean form fades; its nine-minute title is not an equivalence to a longer session for every goal.

**Candidate Wellness assembly, editorial:** keep a push, a pull, a leg pattern and a short core or hinge block; use the app's own ranges and reduce total sets to fit the time available. A dip is optional only when a suitable dip apparatus and comfortable shoulder range are confirmed. If no pull can be set up safely in that window, record the shorter session honestly and schedule pulling separately rather than claiming full-body coverage.

## No-bar home session

**Creator examples, C/A.** [#33, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5nANqBSpZ-g&t=94s) shows easier-angle household rows, then table rows, plus floor Y/T/W work; the table is a potential support only after it is checked. [#66, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IwCyFcL6Tqg&t=28s) combines table rows, push-ups, squats and rear-foot elevated split squats, while specifically warning to check sliding tables and wobbling chairs ([setup check](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=IwCyFcL6Tqg&t=342s)). Neither video proves that ordinary furniture is rated for bodyweight exercise.

**Candidate Wellness assembly, editorial:** pair [P02 incline](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#p02) or [P08 floor push-up](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#p08) with a verified [R26 table row](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#r26) or [R24 door-frame row](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#r24), then [L01 squat](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#l01), [L25 bridge](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#l25), and [C01 plank](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c01). Floor [R32–R34 Y/T/W raises](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#r32) can supplement shoulder/back work; they do not automatically replace a loading row. If there is no suitable row support, show the missing pull slot instead of treating a door or loose table as available equipment.

## Focused practice

Use these as **one optional goal alongside a general program**, with hard skill work when fresh ([#87, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DYIc5Duld38&t=128s)). The numbers below describe the videos' examples, not verified readiness gates.

| Goal | Source-based practice path | What to review before an app suggestion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| First push-up | [#35, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=LYGQ1OVGAjI&t=85s): choose an incline allowing about 8–10 clean reps, three sets two or three times weekly; lower the support after three sets of 12. [#43, guest Hampton](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=MPqezviyQdo&t=55s) also shows wall, incline, knee and mixed full-lowering/knee-press options. | Support height, alignment, wrist comfort and whether the next variation remains controlled; [P01–P08](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#push). |
| First pull-up | [#8, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DdJtd1tRBCI&t=103s): active shoulder setup, then band-assisted complete reps. [#6, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QTeHQFN1fiE&t=221s) adds rows, hangs, scapular pulls and negatives. | Secure overhead bar, any assistance band and its anchor, controlled exit, grip and shoulder response; [R04–R09](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#pull). |
| More pull-ups, with an existing clean rep | [#90, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=yHuiTeCG4AM&t=131s) gives a **five-rep-max example** of six sets of two, progressing submaximal volume with roughly two to five minutes' rest. [#26, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=edcbXUVH8-A&t=105s) offers easy singles before sets of five. | Existing clean maximum, recovery and the actual setup; neither a doubled 30-day result nor a 30-rep target is promised. |
| Pistol squat | [#96, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ZPpti4Xxs_w&t=334s): squat → split squat → assisted single-leg/box work → controlled negative → full movement. | Support stability, balance and comfortable range; [L01–L15](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#legs). |
| Handstand | [#32, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=u-k3gj6Rlhw&t=99s): pike hold and hollow control → wall hold with a practised cartwheel exit → split-leg balance drills. | Clear practice space, wrist/shoulder tolerance, supervision where needed and a controlled exit; [HS01–HS10](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#handstand). |
| Lever, planche or muscle-up | [#3](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5lE8-EAC4mU&t=146s), [#4](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=CHuKBugD8Zk&t=214s), [#58](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qLBPPf9x3GU&t=133s), and [#63, A](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=0r9uswEQwlY&t=90s) give separate staged drills. | Treat each as an expert skill with its own apparatus and exit, not as a required step in general strength work. Do not adopt the back-lever video's suggestion to continue through elbow pain. |

## Conditioning choices

**Creator examples, A.** [#86](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7LSURI1zmU&t=173s) presents short sprints, basic jump rope, and burpees. It suggests 20–30 m sprint repeats with walk-back recovery for people beginning sprint training, 30–60-second basic-bounce rope bouts ([rope](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7LSURI1zmU&t=277s)), and a no-jump burpee before harder versions ([burpee](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=W7LSURI1zmU&t=411s)). [#61, C](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=5PwcgXY2AZk&t=301s) places one or more rope/interval sessions alongside three strength sessions and easier walking or mobility. The [CDC advice to start gradually](https://www.cdc.gov/healthy-weight-growth/physical-activity/getting-started.html) makes all-out sprints a poor unassessed default.

For the app, offer a modest rope bout [CO01](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#co01), a manageable [CO07 no-jump burpee](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#co07), or walking through the existing activity flow before a maximal sprint suggestion. Log work duration or distance, intensity, recovery and actual repetitions where applicable. The channel's 100-squat and 100-burpee challenges ([#23](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=fl50gc1CxjM&t=121s), [#77](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=-ensdxFTLGk&t=214s)) are challenge narratives, not general daily defaults.

## Core and maintenance examples

**Creator maintenance sequence, C.** [#7](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=ygHqLVPANns&t=356s) proposes three sets of 30–60 seconds of [R04 dead hang](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#r04), three sets of 30 seconds of [C01 hollow-braced plank](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c01), and two sets of 60 seconds of [L16 wall sit](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#l16). He presents it as a daily 30-day maintenance challenge. For the app, retain it as a source-labeled optional sequence; do not infer that maximal daily hangs or long holds are appropriate for everyone. A reachable hang duration and shorter controlled holds are valid starting points, and these movements do not replace pulling, pressing and progressive leg work.

**Creator morning core session, A.** [#74](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r5jO5pohbs&t=42s) describes about ten minutes with three sets of 10–15 [C23 crucifix crunches](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c23), then 30 seconds each of [C24 straight-leg hold](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c24), [C05 hollow hold](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c05) and [C25 flutter kicks](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c25), followed by [C26 side-plank hip raises](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#c26) for 30–45 seconds per side ([hold sequence](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r5jO5pohbs&t=165s), [side work](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=4r5jO5pohbs&t=222s)). “No rest” applies to the middle three-movement sequence in the source note, not necessarily to the entire session. Exact movement identification and timing come from local audio recognition; inspect the demonstration before making an app timer. The video's “six-pack” title is not an outcome promise from doing this routine.

## Mobility flow

**Creator example, C.** [#25](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=FMTBN88kDns&t=91s) demonstrates a roughly ten-minute sequence: [M04 downward dog to lunge](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#m04), [M05 kneeling Cossack to hamstring reach](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#m05), [M06 rotating bridge](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#m06), then [M07–M08 seated hip rotation](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-exercise-requirements.md#m07). Move within a comfortable range. The video's daily frequency is its own suggestion, not a treatment schedule. If presented in Wellness, use the Mobility player and its completion bookkeeping, as described in the [integration proposal](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-app-integration.md).

## Editorial decisions before implementation

1. Ask which of these suggestions and catalog exercises the user actually wants; this shortlist does not select all 218 movements for the app.
2. Check any A-sourced exact number or ambiguous variation against the video demonstration before publishing it as app copy. The [Level 3 push-up follow-along #120](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=rDXtJFCtrYE) has insufficient spoken detail for a verified order or dose.
3. Reconcile chosen examples with the existing goal-based ranges, session coverage, rest rules and equipment resolver. Store the actual support, assistance, load, time or distance so later sessions are comparable. The [integration proposal](../../../../soomaries/helth/fitness/yellow-dude-app-integration.md) describes these seams.
4. Keep creator challenges, physique promises, nutrition targets and medical claims out of automatic workout suggestions unless separately reviewed. A suggestion should explain its purpose and how to scale it, without promising a particular outcome or deadline.
